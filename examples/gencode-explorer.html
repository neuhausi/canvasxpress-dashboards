GENCODE — Genome Browser (live)

Pick a gene: a genome browser shows each transcript's exon model plus the GWAS Catalog SNPs in the region. Drag or scroll-wheel over the plot to pan / zoom — it re-queries the region live. Served live from gencode.sqlite via the /api/gencode endpoint in examples/dbservices.py — run examples/serve.py or examples/db-adapter.py (see examples/DB-DASHBOARDS.md).
