CCLE Gene Explorer

The Gene and Data type controls are dashboard parameters that re-query four connector sources (/api/ccle: distribution, heatmap, correlation, mutations). The Gene, X-gene and Y-gene controls autocomplete gene symbols (type-ahead + pick); the heatmap has its own comma-separated gene list; the correlation has four selectors (X gene, X type, Y gene, Y type). Served live from ccle.sqlite.
