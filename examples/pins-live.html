Pins Board (live connector)

The Palmer Penguins table was published to a Posit pins board with pin_write() — exactly what an analyst produces — and is read back here live through the real PinsBoardSource connector (GET /connectors/api/data?source=pins-penguins). Nothing is baked: the server reads the pin, reshapes it, and the browser only sees chart data. You must be signed in to this app (the connector session is bridged from your login). Open it inside the Builder to see the source wiring.
