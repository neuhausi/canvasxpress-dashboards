IBM Options Wall

A single OptionsWall composite over real IBM data — a candlestick price core flanked by put/call walls. Drag the Expiry slider to swap the option chain across 14 expiries, and toggle the Flank metric between implied volatility and premium. Both are mode:"config" controls that drive the panel's live config via updateConfig.
