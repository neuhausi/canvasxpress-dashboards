Cohort Explorer

A 60-patient oncology cohort built from three sources. Tumour expression is joined to clinical data on patient_id, lab visits are related by patient (select patients anywhere to mark their labs), a Filters panel with saved schemes drives every chart, and the gene panel is computed by an R function. Survival and the PD-L1 scatter plot one point per patient straight from the table.
