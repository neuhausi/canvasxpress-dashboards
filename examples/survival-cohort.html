Survival Cohort (Kaplan–Meier)

A synthetic immuno-oncology trial (n=160). A survival table — a time column plus a 0/1 event — renders as a Kaplan–Meier panel with 95% confidence bands, a censor rug, the log-rank p-value and an at-risk table, split by treatment arm, beside a PD-L1 expression box plot.
