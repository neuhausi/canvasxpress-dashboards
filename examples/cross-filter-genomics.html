Cross-Filter — Clinical × Expression

16 patients linked to their expression samples by patient id, with markingMode:"filter". Select responders in the tumor-response scatter and the expression heatmap and PD-L1 boxplot filter to just those patients' samples — select in the heatmap to filter the clinical scatter the other way. A floating Reset cross-filter control clears it.
