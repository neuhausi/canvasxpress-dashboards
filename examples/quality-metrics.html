Manufacturing Quality Metrics

Quality KPIs across four production lines from 500 inspection records: four ring meters — three averages (first-pass yield, defect rate, on-time delivery) plus a total-count (inspections, where All = the sum of the lines), a 500-point yield run chart (one series per line), and a 500-bar defect-rate-per-inspection chart. Two controls — Production Line and Shift — filter the board.
