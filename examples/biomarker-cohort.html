Immuno-Oncology Biomarker Cohort

A 60-sample biomarker study over three datasets: two boxplots with the individual samples overlaid (serum IL-6 by treatment group, TNF-α by response), an IL-6 vs TNF-α correlation scatter, and a 24-gene inflammation-panel heatmap. Two filter controls — Sex and Timepoint — narrow the samples in every panel (kept orthogonal to the boxplot groupings so they subset each box rather than empty it).
