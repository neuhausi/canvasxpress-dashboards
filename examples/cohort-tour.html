Cohort Tour

A 60-patient trial as a guided tour: press play (or the arrows) to walk the whole cohort, the drug arm (a saved filter scheme), a high-risk subgroup (explicit filters) and one patient on the case card. Every step is the live dashboard, so stop anywhere and explore. Click a point to load any other patient.
