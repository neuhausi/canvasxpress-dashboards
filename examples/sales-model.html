Sales Model

Four data-model features on 24 orders and 8 customers, all authored without JSON in the builder: calculated fields on the orders source (Margin, Margin %, a quantile Size band; the Filters panel lists them), a product summary shaped by a group-by that runs in the browser, orders and customers linked by customer (select orders to focus their customers), and orders joined to customers for revenue by segment.
